## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

1.Pre-Order Pools:Customers book crops before they are ready, so farmers know what to grow.
2.Farmer-Owned Micro-Hubs:Farmers work together to store and sell their products.
3.QR Price Transparency:QR codes show how much money goes to farmers and others.
4.AI Demand-Supply Engine:AI helps farmers know what crops to grow and at what price.
5.Adopt-a-Farm Subscription:Families choose a farm and get fresh vegetables every week.

## Technical Approach

1.Pre-Order Pools:Create a website or app to book crops before harvest.
2.Farmer-Owned Micro-Hubs:Use a system to manage crop storage and delivery.
3.QR Price Transparency:Use QR codes to show crop details and prices.
4.AI Demand-Supply Engine:Use AI to predict demand and suggest fair prices.
5.Adopt-a-Farm Subscription:Create an app where families can choose a farm and get vegetables every week.
